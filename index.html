Privacy Policy for Blueprint Notes

Blueprint Notes is a notes app for iPhone and iPad. We do not run a Blueprint Notes account system and we do not operate a server that reads your notes.

  • On your device and iCloud: Notes, photos, recordings, tags, templates, and style presets are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, notes can sync through Apple iCloud (CloudKit). That data belongs to your Apple ID. We cannot open your private iCloud notes. A copy you send with “Send a copy via iCloud” is shared through Apple’s tools with the people you invite. We do not sit in the middle of that share. The optional 7-day organizer trial start date may sync with iCloud Key-Value storage so reinstalling on the same Apple ID does not reset the trial.
  • Purchases: Kitchen, Field, Studio, and Unlock all are one-time In-App Purchases processed entirely by Apple. We do not receive your card number. Purchase status is checked with StoreKit on your device. Family Sharing is not used.
  • Permissions (only when you use the feature): Camera (photo or document scan), Microphone (record audio), Speech Recognition (Apple’s speech service may process audio to transcribe recordings or scans), Photos (library or shared images), Contacts (optional; a name, phone, or email you pick can be stored in a Contact organizer), Maps / place (optional; a location you choose can be stored in a Place organizer). You can deny a permission in iOS Settings.
  • Share extension: A URL, text, or image you share into Blueprint Notes is written to the app group on your device and appended to Today. It is not sent to us.
  • Sync notifications: Apple may use its push infrastructure to keep iCloud notes in sync. We do not save device tokens on our own servers. We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not sell your data or use your notes to train models.
  • Children: Blueprint Notes is not directed at children under 13.

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change. Questions: bncdgames@gmail.com.
