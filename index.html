# Privacy Policy for Blueprint Notes (English - Master) **Last updated:** November 6, 2026 **Hosted at:** https://bazsonagy.github.io/blueprintnote/ Blueprint Notes is an architectural notebook atelier engineered for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when using Blueprint Notes. We operate on a zero-knowledge, hardware-isolated architecture: we do not maintain a user account system, and we do not operate servers that read, store, transmit, or monetize your notes. ### 1. What Stays on Your Device & In Your Private iCloud - **Local Storage**: Notes, handwritten canvas strokes, photographs, audio recordings, hashtags, and organizer blocks are stored securely in local app storage on your device. - **Apple iCloud (CloudKit) Sync**: When signed in to your Apple ID with iCloud enabled, Blueprint Notes syncs through Apple's CloudKit private database. That data is tied to your personal Apple ID and encrypted by Apple. We have zero access to your private database or its contents. - **Shared Notes**: If you explicitly share a note via Apple’s native iCloud sharing tools, Apple manages the transmission directly with the recipients you invite. We do not intermediate or log that exchange. ### 2. 100% On-Device Artificial Intelligence Blueprint Notes features integrated local artificial intelligence (such as Qwen 2.5 and Llama 3.2 architectures) running directly on Apple Silicon: - **Local Processing**: AI features—including Meeting Digest synthesis, SWOT Analysis extraction, Chronology sequencing, and text restructuring—execute 100% offline within your device's memory. - **No Cloud Inference**: No prompts, note selections, or generated text are transmitted across the internet to OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or developer servers. - **Model Training**: Your content is never collected, stored, or utilized to train or fine-tune artificial intelligence models. ### 3. Document Scanner & Audio Memos - **Camera & Document Scanner**: Used strictly to capture physical documents, sketches, or receipts directly into a note. Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and text cleaning run entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework and local neural models. - **Microphone & Voice Recordings**: Audio recordings are saved in the app's sandboxed storage. Speech recognition operates on-device using Apple's system speech frameworks; no audio data is sent to external speech servers. ### 4. Permissions (Granted Only on Demand) The app requests system permissions only when you explicitly invoke a feature: - **Camera**: Attach photographs or scan documents into a note. - **Microphone**: Record voice memos into an audio card. - **Speech Recognition**: Transcribe recorded audio on-device. - **Photo Library**: Select and attach images from your Photos library. - **Contacts (Optional)**: If you add a Contact organizer, you may pick a contact to store their name, phone, or email within that specific note. - **Location (Optional)**: If you add a Place organizer, you may select a location to display a map pin. You can revoke permissions at any time in iOS/iPadOS/macOS System Settings. ### 5. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2) In-App Purchases (such as Unlimited Notes, Color Packs, and Organizer Bundles) are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive, store, or process your credit card numbers or billing addresses. Purchase entitlements are cryptographically verified on-device via Apple StoreKit receipts. ### 6. What We Do Not Collect - We do not use third-party analytics (e.g., Firebase, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, AppsFlyer). - We do not use advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, AppLovin). - We do not track you across other apps or websites (ATT tracking is not used). - We do not collect device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV) for profiling. ### 7. Children's Privacy Blueprint Notes is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. ### 8.
